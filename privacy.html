Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

These tools are used by one person: me, Clyde Palmer. They only ever sign in to my own Google account. This page explains what they do with the information that gives them.

What the tools can access

  • Gmail: they read messages so I can find what needs a reply, create drafts, and send the daily report to the clients I work with. My personal assistant also files my mail into labels, archives what's dealt with, and emails a daily briefing to me (only ever to my own address). They never delete email.
  • Google Sheets: they read and update sheets I own or that have been shared with me.
  • Google Calendar: my personal assistant reads my calendars to see when I'm free, and adds reminders and meetings to my own calendar.

AI processing

My personal assistant uses Anthropic's Claude AI model, under my own Anthropic account, to sort my mail, spot deadlines and write draft replies for me to check. The text of the emails it works on is sent to Claude for that purpose only. Short summaries of what it finds are sent to me, in my own email and my own Slack messages.

How it's used

The information is only used to do the jobs above, for my own work. It isn't sold, it isn't used for advertising, and it isn't shared with anyone apart from the people a message is addressed to.

Where it's kept

The sign-in tokens are kept on my own computer, or in Secret Manager in my own Google Cloud project, where only the job that needs a token can read it. Message content is processed as it's needed and isn't kept apart from the drafts and sent mail in my own mailbox.

Google API Services User Data Policy

These tools' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
